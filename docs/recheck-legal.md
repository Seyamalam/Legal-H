# Independent legal recheck

Reviewed on 5 September 2026 against the exact questions, answers, research notes and source registers for Challenges 1 and 4. This review used fresh official web retrieval and visual inspection of the cached Bangla Gazette pages. It found one procedural wording correction worth making; the central legal conclusions remain supported.

The notice-sequence correction has been applied to the answer and research notes. A follow-up reading of the revised wording found no material regression. The findings below retain the reasoning behind that correction.

## Recommended correction to Challenge 4

The version reviewed used "After safety screening and required consents" before mediator assignment and notices. Rule 4 distinguishes applicant consent, opening the file and notice from the opponent's later consent and the decision to conduct or assign mediation. The recommended replacement is:

> After safety screening, the officer obtains applicant consent, opens the file and arranges lawful notices. Once the opponent consents, an authorised, conflict-free mediator conducts the consented virtual process.

This describes the ordinary consenting route. The separate stop paragraph must continue to address unsafe participation and mandatory initiation. Refusal or nonappearance goes to the officer for the prescribed procedure. ## Challenge 1: change and section reference

Section 24 of Act No. 45 of 2026 replaces principal section 16. The answer correctly identifies direct and online application to the specified legal aid offices, and separates a proposed voice workflow from the existing form. The Gazette was visually checked at PDF page 18, printed page 15734. It does not establish that the web service first existed in April 2026. [2026 amendment Gazette](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/61406_96331.pdf).

A fresh historical check strengthens the comparison: the official judiciary-hosted copy of Ordinance No. 35 of 2025 contains no amendment of section 16. Its virtual-mediation definition and sections 21B and 21C therefore must not be mistaken for a 2025 amendment authorising online applications in section 16. The answer does not claim that earlier online filing was unlawful. [Official 2025 ordinance copy, all eight pages](https://netrakona.judiciary.gov.bd/storage/app/uploads/public/689/9f6/6ac/6899f66accdbc528944265.pdf).

## Challenge 4: legal safeguards checked

The stop condition correctly distinguishes consent to a virtual meeting from refusal of mediation generally. Rule 3's eligibility consequences and mandatory-initiation provision require human assessment. The answer does not invent a domestic-violence exemption. Rules 13 and 19 support its execution safeguards: signatures, certification and retained photographs matter, while the special overseas execution provisions cannot simply be applied to a party in Gazipur. Research notes correctly state rule 9's ordinary 21-working-day period from the first meeting, the qualified additional seven working days and the separate adjournment limit. [2025 rules, PDF pages 2-3 and 5-8](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2024/12/5038ecb1d58c4ecaba546d5db0c62254.pdf).

The commencement account is also supported. Joypurhat is named for section 21B and Schedule items 1-6 and 8 from 25 January 2026. The later notice adds items 7 and 9 from 31 July 2026. Section 21C has a distinct nationwide commencement instrument. These are legal coverage findings, not proof that Joypurhat has a functioning ODR booth. [January Gazette](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/0/5b8a761e-9139-4b93-b806-93926b261a19.pdf), [August Gazette](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/7/0b8c8545-8ee3-461c-a907-8e943ae5058c.pdf), [September 2025 replacement notice](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/0/8456608f-0bb6-49f9-acae-0129de28fcc4.pdf).
