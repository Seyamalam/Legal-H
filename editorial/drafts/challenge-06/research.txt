# Research notes: call centre and omnichannel support

Reviewed on 5 September 2026. This file separates observed service features from proposals. Source details and verification limits appear in [sources.md](sources.md).

## The specific barrier to use in the answer

The live online application contains a text area labelled "General Description (সাধারন বর্ণনা)". Ripon must turn his spoken account into written form content, then verify what the entrepreneur entered. His blindness, the case's reported Bangla screen-reader limitations and lack of usable internet make that step difficult. Assistance with typing alone does not give him an independent way to check the account. The field's existence is verified; the difficulty follows from the supplied case. [Official application](https://db.nlaso.gov.bd/Pages/OnlineApplications.aspx), source Q6-S1.

This is a stronger basis than a claimed CAPTCHA or mandatory NID upload. Neither was established by this inspection. It also keeps the answer focused on one element rather than listing every possible problem with the website.

## What the public application establishes

The DBLA homepage links "অনলাইন আবেদন" to the application on `db.nlaso.gov.bd`. The legacy domain is therefore still the route promoted by the current department. The homepage identifies 16699 as the national legal-aid call centre. [DBLA homepage](https://dbla.gov.bd/), Q6-S2.

The public form separates applicant and representative details. It marks the applicant contact-number label with an asterisk, while its applicant NID label has no asterisk. Its general-description text area has an adjacent HTML label without a `for` attribute; the text area has no `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` in the retrieved markup. This is a limited markup observation, not a complete screen-reader test. No application was submitted and no server validation or notification was triggered. [Official application](https://db.nlaso.gov.bd/Pages/OnlineApplications.aspx), Q6-S1.

A representative section is evidence that the interface can record a representative. It does not establish that a brother can consent for an adult woman, sign every required document, or receive every case detail without her authority. An asterisk is a visual cue, not proof of a statutory requirement. An absent asterisk does not establish that an office must waive identification.

## Requirements the proposal must preserve

The July 2026 district charter lists voter ID and relevant papers for district-office legal advice. For publicly funded litigation assistance it lists an income certificate, birth certificate/voter ID and case documents. The mediation row also permits birth certificate/voter ID. Page 11 directs people who cannot read to ask the office assistant for help filling the application. It gives office hours as Sunday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. [District charter, pages 1, 2 and 11](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/6/a84db22f-9b31-4abd-a32d-c01e51460d62.pdf), Q6-S3.

The service rows differ. Do not collapse them into either "NID is always mandatory" or "no ID is required." Treat the withheld NID as information for an officer to resolve through the applicable service's requirements and accepted alternatives. Do not invent a replacement identity number. A recorded enquiry or incomplete draft is not automatically a formally accepted legal-aid application.

## What the helpline currently promises

The June 2026 central charter describes toll-free incoming calls to 16699 for legal advice and information, without charge, with an immediate service standard. It separately lists online advice through the website, Facebook Messenger and SMS. The charter does not promise complete voice application filing or a common cross-channel case record. [Central charter, page 2](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/6/bcfb1eb6-07c3-4a05-a4fe-63f2353914aa.pdf), Q6-S4.

The official helpline poster says 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. It does not state which days. It advertises advice, legal-aid information, counselling, preliminary information about proceedings, and advice or complaints about government legal services. [DBLA helpline page and poster](https://dbla.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/69ca4e8234fc363bf320ca55), Q6-S5.

Moyuri's Friday window therefore creates a real delivery question. A proposal must arrange a staffed safe-time appointment, or establish another safe route with her. It cannot assume that existing office hours cover Friday or claim that the helpline operates around the clock. An urgent-safety route should not depend on completion of an application.

## One proposed improvement

Add a helpline-assisted voice-to-form service for that description field, using the same draft record at the call centre and an authorised Union Digital Centre workstation. This is a proposed service extension, not a claim about an existing integration.

1. Ripon calls 16699 from a phone he can safely use. A trained operator asks brief questions in Bangla and records Moyuri as the person seeking aid, with Ripon identified separately as the source and proposed representative. AI may suggest a transcript or draft description. A human operator remains available when recognition fails.
2. The operator reads the description back in Bangla, one short section at a time. Ripon can correct names, dates, uncertainty and allegations orally. Unknown facts remain unknown. The operator checks and accepts each correction before adding the text to the form.
3. Staff retain the same substantive application information, evidence and authorisation requirements. They explain missing items orally and refer questions about identity alternatives, consent and acceptance to the responsible legal-aid officer. The service must not turn a draft into a filed or approved application merely because AI completed the text.
4. A short reference is spoken aloud and repeated for recall. It helps staff find the draft, but does not by itself authenticate a caller or authorise disclosure. A recorded consent process defines what Ripon may do and what information he may receive. Staff seek Moyuri's own instructions through a safe arrangement.
5. The record carries a contact restriction: no SMS, OTP, email, automated call or voicemail to Moyuri's household handset. Ripon retrieves permitted updates by calling in, using a separate verification method agreed orally. Staff do not send even a neutral message unless the chosen recipient and channel are safe and authorised. A callback to Ripon also needs permission; a tea-stall number is not automatically a safe private contact.

The shared draft is how this remains one improvement across channels. Adding an app, chatbot, USSD flow and document wallet as separate solutions would make the 250-word answer harder to defend.

## Three-condition check

| Condition in the question | How the proposed improvement meets it | Remaining dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Citizen has no working internet | A conventional voice call reaches staff who enter the description. | Voice coverage and a staffed service still have to exist. |
| Person filing cannot read a screen | Staff ask questions and read every description segment aloud in Bangla. Corrections and references are spoken. | Testing must include blind Bangla-speaking users and local accents. |
| Nothing may be pushed to Moyuri's handset | A contact restriction applies across the draft, case system, call centre and notification service; authorised users retrieve updates by calling. | Staff must verify that every downstream channel honours the restriction. |

If the operator's internet also fails, staff need a secure pending-draft queue and must tell the caller that submission is pending. That fallback is an implementation proposal. It does not justify silently caching intimate allegations on a shared entrepreneur's personal phone. Any local storage needs restricted access, encryption and deletion after confirmed transfer.

## What still requires operational validation

- Whether DBLA permits trained call-centre staff to capture and submit an application on a person's instructions, and which authentication or signature steps remain necessary.
- The current helpline's working days and ability to arrange a Friday appointment.
- Whether the live system accepts missing NID at submission, which later documents staff request, and which identity alternatives apply to the particular service.
- Whether the current workflow sends automatic notices and whether every such notice can honour a contact restriction.
- Whether existing Union Digital Centres can access drafts with limited permissions, provide a private interview and avoid retaining copies.
- Whether the form works with TalkBack, Bangla voices, keyboard navigation and an actual low-cost phone. The supplied case describes one user's experience; this review did not reproduce it on that handset.

These are specific checks for implementation, not evidence that the service has already failed them. No real person was contacted, no test case was filed, and no visit to the Joypurhat office occurred.
