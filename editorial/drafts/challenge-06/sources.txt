# Sources: call centre and omnichannel support

All sources below are official primary sources. Access date: 5 September 2026. English descriptions are working translations or summaries. Page references are one-based PDF pages. Publication dates come from the portal records and may differ from the date of an underlying document.

## Q6-S1. Online application

- Title: DBLA | Office Management System, online application.
- Publisher: Bangladesh Legal Aid Department, hosted on the legacy NLASO database domain.
- URL: [Public application](https://db.nlaso.gov.bd/Pages/OnlineApplications.aspx).
- Publication/update date: no date shown on the form.
- Relevant location: first general-information panel; applicant-information panel; representative-details section.
- Verified: the text area for the general description, contact-number asterisk, unstarred applicant NID field and separate representative fields. Retrieved public HTML confirms the description control is a text area and its adjacent label has no explicit programmatic association.
- Method: read the live page through web retrieval and inspect a fresh unauthenticated HTML GET. No data entry or submission.
- Limits: did not exercise dynamic steps, server validation, an actual screen reader, staff processing or notifications. Do not claim that CAPTCHA/OTP exists, NID is mandatory online, or absence of an asterisk waives later verification.

## Q6-S3. District legal-aid office citizen charter

- Title: মাঠ পর্যায়ের সেবা প্রদান প্রতিশ্রুতি (Citizen's Charter), listed as সিটিজেনস চার্টার (জেলা লিগ্যাল এইড অফিস).
- Publisher: Bangladesh Legal Aid Department.
- Discovery page: [Current charter list](https://dbla.gov.bd/pages/office-citizen-charters).
- Document: [District charter PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/6/a84db22f-9b31-4abd-a32d-c01e51460d62.pdf).
- Portal publication date: 21 July 2026. Detail page updated 22 July 2026, 16:34.
- Relevant location: pages 1 and 2, citizen services rows 1 to 3; page 11, working hours and applicant instructions item 4. Page 5 contains the Joypurhat office contacts.
- Supports: documentary requirements differ by service; office-assisted form completion already has a published basis; district office hours do not cover Friday.
- Method: reviewed extracted text and visually checked rendered pages 1, 2 and 11 because Bangla text extraction distorted some characters.
- Limits: a service charter describes administrative expectations; it is not proof of every statutory requirement, every exception or every local practice. No contact number was called.

## Q6-S4. Central citizen charter

- Title: সেবা প্রদান প্রতিশ্রুতি (সিটিজেনস চার্টার), listed as সিটিজেনস চার্টার (বাংলাদেশ আইনগত সহায়তা অধিদপ্তর).
- Publisher: Bangladesh Legal Aid Department.
- Discovery page: [Current charter list](https://dbla.gov.bd/pages/office-citizen-charters).
- Document: [Central charter PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/6/bcfb1eb6-07c3-4a05-a4fe-63f2353914aa.pdf).
- Portal publication date: 21 June 2026. Detail page updated 21 July 2026, 14:09.
- Relevant location: page 2, citizen services rows 2 and 3.
- Supports: toll-free incoming-call legal advice at 16699; a free, immediate service standard; separate online advice channels.
- Method: extracted text and visual check of rendered page 2.
- Limits: does not establish complete telephone application filing, integration of all channels, notification suppression or 24-hour operation. An immediate service standard is not a measured waiting-time guarantee.

- Title: জাতীয় হেল্পলাইন ১৬৬৯৯.
- Publisher: Bangladesh Legal Aid Department.
- Page: [National helpline service page](https://dbla.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/69ca4e8234fc363bf320ca55).

- Content update displayed: 30 March 2026, 16:20.
- Relevant location: yellow opening-hours oval, helpline number and listed services.
- Supports: the poster advertises 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., toll-free domestic calls, legal advice and related information or complaints.
- Method: visually inspected the original poster. The page's extracted text alone does not contain the image's opening hours.
