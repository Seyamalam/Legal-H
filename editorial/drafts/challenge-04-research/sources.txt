# Sources for challenge 4

Access date: 5 September 2026. English explanations of Bangla legislation are research translations and paraphrases. The original Bangla text controls. Official PDFs were downloaded and the cited pages visually checked because several text layers use corrupt or legacy Bangla encodings.

## C4-S1: Principal Act and 2026 amendment

Publisher: Bangladesh Parliament and BD Laws; consolidated copy distributed by DBLA.

- [DBLA laws index](https://nlaso.gov.bd/pages/laws)
- [Consolidated Act, print dated 4 May 2026](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/4/1c316c8c-ff1a-4cfa-863b-106072b00b0e.pdf)
- [Act No. 45 of 2026, gazette of 10 April 2026](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/61406_96331.pdf)

Pinpoints in the consolidation: section 2(ড), PDF p2, physical or virtual participation; section 21A, p14, officers' functions; section 21B, p14, scheduled pre-case initiation, report for later filing, limitation and district jurisdiction; section 21C, p15, signed/certified agreements and enforcement; section 21D, p15, assistance for special measures. Pinpoints in the amending Act: section 1, p1, commencement; section 34, pp22-23, Schedule; section 35, p23, repeal of earlier ordinances and saving of notifications and actions.

The Schedule names Family Courts Act section 5 matters at item 1, Dowry Prohibition Act sections 3 and 4 at item 8, and Women and Children Repression Prevention Act section 11(গ) at item 9. Inclusion does not establish when each item became operative in a particular district.

## C4-S2: January commencement for Joypurhat

Publisher: Law and Justice Division, Bangladesh Gazette Extraordinary, 20 January 2026. SRO No. 25-Law/2026, dated 19 January 2026.

[Official one-page gazette](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/0/5b8a761e-9139-4b93-b806-93926b261a19.pdf)

The notification activates section 21B and Schedule items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 from 25 January 2026 in eight districts, expressly including Joypurhat. Its authority refers to the 2025 amendment ordinance. The later 2026 Act's section 35 saves prior notifications. It supports the territorial relevance of maintenance and dowry-demand proceedings here; it does not make all domestic violence an identical category.

## C4-S3: Latest expansion reviewed

Publisher: Law and Justice Division, Bangladesh Gazette Extraordinary, 2 August 2026. SRO No. 288-Law/2026, dated 30 July 2026.

[Official one-page gazette](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/7/0b8c8545-8ee3-461c-a907-8e943ae5058c.pdf)

Activates section 21B and Schedule items 7 and 9 from 31 July 2026 across thirty named districts, including Joypurhat. Item 7 concerns a specified cheque category; item 9 concerns section 11(গ) dowry-related hurt. The file's gazette date, notification date, effective date and portal upload date are different.

For comparison, [SRO 276-Law/2026](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/6/9ee3275f-49f5-47bb-ab7f-0dbec5c2a2fe.jpeg), dated 5 July and gazetted 7 July, adds ten other districts for items 1-6 and 8 from 21 July. The [September 2025 replacement notification, SRO 382](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/0/8456608f-0bb6-49f9-acae-0129de28fcc4.pdf) applies section 21C in all districts from 18 September 2025, and supersedes SRO 366. These documents prevent an inaccurate claim that every scheduled matter became mandatory nationwide at once.

## C4-S4: Legal advice and mediation rules, 2025

Publisher: Law and Justice Division, Bangladesh Gazette Extraordinary, 17 September 2025; SRO 372-Law/2025, notification dated 16 September; printed pp9237-9248. DBLA lists the rules on its legislative-information page.

- [DBLA rules page](https://nlaso.gov.bd/pages/legislative-informations/আইনগত-সহায়তা-প্রদান-আইনি-পরামর্শ-ও-মধ্যস্থতা-বিধিমালা-২০২৫-8179aa-6922da35933eb65569e031b5)
- [Official rules PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2024/12/5038ecb1d58c4ecaba546d5db0c62254.pdf)

| Rules | PDF pages | What they establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2, 3 | 1-3 | Audio or video can be virtual presence; legal advice; consent and refusal provisions; mandatory initiation for scheduled matters. |
| 4, 5, 6, 7 | 3-4 | Intake consent, separate opponent consent, opening a file, lawful service, notices and repeat notices. Rule 5 includes voice communication as an available electronic method in necessary cases. |
| 8, 9 | 5-6 | Separate discussions, attendance and signatures; adjournment and completion limits. |
| 10, 11 | 6-7 | Mediator conflicts, respectful and sensitive treatment, no pressure to accept the mediator's proposal, and special measures. |
| 12, 13 | 7 | Formal nomination of a lawful representative; agreement, signatures, seal, Chief Legal Aid Officer certification and photographs. |
| 14, 15, 16 | 7-8 | Failed-mediation report, copies on request, confidentiality and restrictions on later evidential use. |
| 18, 19 | 8-9 | Enforcement route; party consent to virtual meetings; identification; special overseas execution provisions; confidentiality and publication restrictions. |
| 20, 23, 24 | 9-10 | Payment terms and records, limitation calculation and execution applications. |
| 30, 31, 32 | 12 | Record custody, retention, reporting, and replacement of the 2015 mediation rules. |

Limits: the rules do not make an audio recording or a typed name a universally sufficient domestic signature. The special execution methods in rule 19(3)-(4) refer to a party abroad; Sohel being in Gazipur does not satisfy that condition. Rule 3's refusal provisions require close human legal assessment, not automated eligibility consequences.

## C4-S5: DBLA permission for an ODR pilot

Publisher: DBLA. Notice No. 145, letter dated 16 April 2026; portal publication 20 April 2026.

- [Official notice page](https://dbla.gov.bd/pages/notices/নোটিশ-নং-১৪৫-খুলনা-জেলার-পাইকগাছা-চৌকি-আদালতে-online-dispute-resolution-odr-কার্যক্রম-চালু-করার-অনুমতি-প্রদান-প্রসঙ্গে-virtb0-69e5d75d4366ea370797197f)
- [Official notice PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/3/5f5666b4-99d8-4e4b-9137-8b09970cd3a9.pdf)

Page 1 authorizes a pilot at Paikgacha chowki court, Khulna. It requires compliance with the Act, 2025 rules and existing law; coordination with the judge and Khulna DLAO; confidentiality and security of litigant data; quarterly reporting; and permits revocation. Friendship is responsible for the booth's resources and specified data safeguards.

This is concrete DBLA ODR material. It does not prove operational coverage in Joypurhat, safety for this family, or authority to bypass the rules.

## C4-S6: Family and dowry legislation

Official provisions and dates are documented in the [supporting family-law review](../../docs/supporting-family-law.md). Sources include the [Family Courts Act, 2023 gazette](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/50340_46458.pdf), [Dowry Prohibition Act official English text](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-legislativediv/2024/12/049530ae1cf74e4a91122d91eca802a0.pdf), and [Women and Children Repression Prevention amendment, Act 85 of 2026](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/61238_32270.pdf).

Use: distinguish maintenance jurisdiction, a criminal demand for dowry, a void promise to pay dowry, and dowry-related simple hurt. Compoundability is a legal classification; it does not prove safe or voluntary settlement in this case.
