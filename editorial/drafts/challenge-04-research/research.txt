# Research findings: mediation and accountability

Reviewed on 5 September 2026. References C4-S1 to C4-S7 point to the [source register](sources.md). The legal statements summarize official Bangla documents. The proposed digital process is our design, not a claim that the current Joypurhat office operates it.

## The decision this case needs first

Do not move Ripon's report straight into a joint call with Sohel. An officer must first establish a safe way to hear Moyuri, assess immediate harm and coercion, distinguish the legal issues, and check authority for each step. Her short Friday window is a reported opportunity for a private conversation, not verified consent or proof that an appointment would be safe. The process must not notify the household merely because its address or phone appears in an application.

If privacy and freely given consent to the virtual format cannot be established, stop the online route and refer to a human for protection and an appropriate alternative. This does not mean declaring that all compulsory pre-case procedures disappear. Rules 3 and 19 must be read separately and together, as explained below. [C4-S4](sources.md)

## The current legal framework

The 2026 amending Act is Act No. 45, published on 10 April 2026. Section 35 repeals the earlier 2025 and 2026 ordinances but saves notifications, measures and proceedings. The principal Act's section 2(ড) defines mediation to include physical or virtual participation. Section 21A gives officers specified advice and mediation functions. Section 21B requires an initial mediation application for scheduled matters where operative, a prescribed report for subsequent court filing after failure, and exclusion of mediation time in the relevant limitation calculation. Section 21C gives a properly signed and certified agreement binding force and permits enforcement through the competent court. [C4-S1](sources.md)

The power to meet virtually is not a finding that every dispute is suitable for mediation. Neither a smartphone, an audio recording, a typed name, nor a status marked "settled" completes the legal formalities by itself.

## Joypurhat commencement matters

The January notification expressly includes Joypurhat for section 21B and Schedule items 1-6 and 8 from 25 January 2026. Item 1 includes Family Courts Act section 5 matters, which cover maintenance; item 8 concerns Dowry Prohibition Act sections 3 and 4. The August gazette adds items 7 and 9 for thirty districts including Joypurhat from 31 July 2026. Item 9 concerns dowry-related simple hurt under section 11(গ) of the Women and Children Repression Prevention Act. Section 21C's nationwide commencement appears in the September 2025 replacement notification and is preserved by the 2026 saving clause. [C4-S1 to C4-S3](sources.md)

Three dates often differ: the date an instrument is signed, its gazette publication, and its operative date. The DBLA portal's upload date is a fourth. Use the instrument's operative provision. Do not infer that the eight ADLASB project districts are the same as an eight-district statutory notification, or that a thirty-district notice activates every provision nationwide.

The relevant office must still determine jurisdiction and the proper classification of the claim. The husband's work in Gazipur does not convert him into a party living abroad or automatically transfer all responsibility from Joypurhat. The Family Courts Act's venue rule for a wife's maintenance proceedings and the legal-aid officer's jurisdiction are related but separate questions. [C4-S1, C4-S6](sources.md)

## Maintenance, dowry and abuse must remain distinct

Maintenance is a Family Court matter under section 5(ঘ) of the Family Courts Act, 2023. A demand for a motorcycle as a condition of continuing the marriage may fall within the Dowry Prohibition Act. Section 3 makes demanding dowry an offence; section 5 makes a dowry agreement void; section 7 makes the offences compoundable as well as cognizable and non-bailable. The office must not turn an unlawful demand into a negotiable debt or make support for a child depend on paying it. [C4-S6](sources.md)

Injury caused for dowry has separate offence elements. The Women and Children Repression Prevention amendment of 2026 addresses simple hurt under section 11(গ), with a deemed commencement date of 25 March 2025 and procedural exceptions. The fact that some offences are compoundable does not mean that every beating, serious injury or other alleged crime can be settled in the same way. A lawyer must assess the connection to dowry, the injury classification, forum, pending proceedings, and lawful route. Read the [supporting family-law notes](../../docs/supporting-family-law.md) for the exact provisions.

Nothing in Ripon's initial report proves all elements of an offence or supplies Moyuri's instructions. A system can organize the issues for review, but cannot convict Sohel, decide a legal remedy, or promise that a settlement disposes of all allegations.

## The difficult consent and refusal provisions

Rule 3(4) states a consequence for an applicant who does not consent to advised mediation: disposition as ineligible for later legal aid in that dispute. Rule 3(5), notwithstanding that provision, directs mandatory initiation for scheduled disputes. Rule 4 provides for consent and a failure report where the opponent does not consent or attend; its proviso addresses later aid eligibility where that opponent lacks a proper reason. These provisions prevent a blanket promise that refusing mediation can never affect aid. [C4-S4, PDF pp2-3](sources.md)

Rule 19(1) separately makes a virtual meeting subject to party consent and consideration of necessity. Rule 10 prohibits pressure to accept the mediator's proposal and requires sensitive treatment of women and marginalized groups. Read together, they support distinguishing an unsafe virtual format from refusal of mediation as a whole. That distinction is an interpretation for this design, not an express statutory domestic-violence exemption. [C4-S4, PDF pp6,8](sources.md)

The system should record "unsafe virtual channel; human alternative required" and preserve the actual explanation. A human determines what mandatory initiation, report, review or alternative procedure applies. The software must not automatically recode a dropped call, unsafe household access, lack of NID or objection to video as refusal, absence without cause, or ineligibility. It should not fabricate a failed-mediation report before the legally required steps occur.

## What DBLA's ODR activity proves

DBLA's April 2026 notice authorizes a pilot at Paikgacha chowki court in Khulna. It requires the Act and 2025 rules to be followed, coordination with the court and district officer, confidentiality and security, and periodic reporting. Approval is revocable. This provides a concrete official example of human-supervised ODR with local support. It does not demonstrate nationwide deployment, a working Joypurhat booth, or safety for this scenario. [C4-S5](sources.md)

The proposed process should use that level of accountability: a named responsible office, documented decisions and a support location checked for privacy. The software vendor or entrepreneur does not become the mediator simply by providing the call or device.

## Minimum proposed process

### 1. Citizen entry and protected intake

Accept Ripon's call through a Bangla voice route or assisted entry. Create a provisional record with a reference, intake date, source-labelled allegations and missing information. Separate the reporter, applicant, proposed representative and contact person. Restrict the household address and private contact note. Explain what the record does and does not mean.

An officer arranges a safe way to consult Moyuri, assesses urgent protection needs, confirms identity and any necessary proxy authority, and applies the service's eligibility and documentary requirements. Rule 19(2) identifies documents and contact information for virtual identification. The charter lists service-specific documents. Staff must resolve withheld identity papers through a lawful procedure, not invent an NID or silently waive identification. [C4-S4, C4-S7](sources.md)

Moyuri's Friday availability may require a specifically staffed appointment or another safe arrangement; current Friday helpline coverage was not verified. A telephone route can work without mobile data on the citizen's handset, but still depends on voice coverage. If neither voice nor data is available, staff must arrange a safe physical alternative rather than classify her as uncooperative.

### 2. Human screening and notice

The officer identifies which issues can lawfully enter the process and records the applicable notification and jurisdiction. Obtain the required participation and virtual-format consent, check conflicts and lawful representation, and assign the officer or authorized mediator. Rule 12 requires nomination of a lawful representative in the prescribed form; being a brother is not a substitute. [C4-S4](sources.md)

Notice is a legal step with its own record. Rule 5 permits several service methods, including voice communication in necessary cases; it does not authorize software to disregard all service requirements. The officer chooses a lawful method with a safety plan. An opponent will learn about the dispute if the process reaches notice. The system cannot truthfully promise that mediation will remain secret from Sohel indefinitely. It can keep Moyuri's private contact window out of invitations and prevent an uncontrolled message from causing premature disclosure.

### 3. Assisted remote mediation

Use short, scheduled audio sessions when appropriate; the rules' definition of virtual presence includes audio. A video-only product would add an unnecessary access barrier. Staff support both ends, privately verify who is present and confirm whether the person can speak freely. Separate discussions are permitted by rule 8(1)(ঘ). Keep confidential caucus information out of the other party's view. [C4-S4, PDF pp2,5](sources.md)

Record attendance and procedural actions. Pause if the connection fails, someone enters the room or a person cannot follow the discussion. Resume only through an agreed safe arrangement. Do not infer consent from silence or auto-dial the household after a dropped call. A later session must fit the statutory timetable: rule 9 ordinarily requires completion within 21 working days from the first meeting and permits up to 7 additional working days for proper reasons with the parties' consent. It also addresses adjournments of no more than seven days. The officer tracks these clocks separately from safe-contact preferences. [C4-S4, PDF pp5-6](sources.md)

### 4. A lawful and understandable outcome

A successful agreement must identify lawful terms, obligations and dates. Explain them in Bangla and read the complete proposed agreement to each person who cannot read it. Ripon may hear only what his authority permits; his read-back does not replace Moyuri's consent. Check that support terms do not conceal a dowry obligation or an unauthorized waiver of other claims.

Rule 13 requires the prescribed agreement, signatures of parties or applicable representatives and witnesses present, the mediator's signature and seal, Chief Legal Aid Officer certification, and preservation of photographs of signing parties and witnesses. The minimum defensible proposal is staff-assisted execution through a method the officer confirms is legally accepted, with necessary originals retained and controlled digital copies linked to the record. For domestic parties, do not rely on rule 19's overseas consular or electronic-signature provisions. If a digital method lacks a verified legal basis, use a safe supported physical step before certification. [C4-S4, PDF pp7-8](sources.md)

Section 21C then gives the properly executed and certified outcome its specified force. The software should show separate statuses for draft terms, signatures outstanding, certification pending and certified outcome. A PDF generated by AI is not a decree merely because its heading says agreement. [C4-S1](sources.md)

If mediation fails, the mediator prepares and signs the prescribed reasoned report under rule 14. Section 21B(3) requires the report for subsequent filing in the covered situation. Rule 15 provides copies on request within three working days. Record lawful failure or closure reasons without disclosing confidential negotiating statements beyond what the prescribed process permits. [C4-S1, C4-S4](sources.md)

### 5. Record, payment and follow-up

Preserve the case reference, source history, consent and authorization records, notices and service proof, attendance, the signed agreement or failure report, certification status and a named follow-up owner. Restrict sensitive fields and record who reads or changes them. Rules 16 and 19 protect confidentiality, including limits on publication and later evidential use of mediation material. Recordkeeping does not require recording every conversation. [C4-S4](sources.md)

Rule 20 requires the amount and time of payment in words and figures and addresses receipts/registers; the agency may prescribe a digital-payment method. Therefore do not promise a new payment channel without checking agency instructions and Moyuri's safe access to funds. The proposed record can track obligations, due dates and confirmed receipts regardless of the authorized payment method.

Apply rule 30's record classes rather than invent a universal retention period: files with agreements follow the period the agency specifies; report-concluded files have the stated one-year retention rule, followed by prescribed destruction recording. Rule 31 addresses periodic reports and digital archives. Any applicable later direction or lawful hold needs officer review. A public transparency dashboard should contain aggregate service information, not case narratives or safe-contact times. [C4-S4, PDF p12](sources.md)

Follow-up must use the verified contact plan. A missed payment creates a task for human review, not automatic household reminders or a new settlement. Section 21C and rules 18 and 24 address enforcement through the competent court and must guide the officer's next step. Do not reduce those provisions to an invented automatic deduction or a universal new sixty-day enforcement limitation. [C4-S1, C4-S4](sources.md)

## Stop condition and route out

The minimum stop condition is inability to establish private, unpressured participation by Moyuri in the virtual format. Immediate danger, surveillance or coercion is sufficient to stop that online session. Keep the application and reasons, alert the responsible officer, and arrange safety assistance and a lawful alternative. Where applicable, the officer considers special measures under section 21D and rule 11 and routes protection or criminal issues to the proper service. Declining unsafe technology should not itself generate an automated adverse legal-aid decision.

There is tension between survivor-centered practice and the legal consequences in the refusal rules. The answer should acknowledge that tension through its routing and documentation, instead of pretending the system can solve it by a consent checkbox. The lawyer must determine the lawful next step for the actual facts.

## Remaining implementation questions

The research does not establish Joypurhat's current ODR staffing, approved remote identity alternatives, domestic execution methods, local forms, payment instructions, or a safe nearby support location for Moyuri. These are implementation checks. The legal and design answer can still specify who must resolve them and block invalid progression until that happens.
