# Sources: AI voice intake

All sources accessed on 5 September 2026. Page numbers are printed pages unless specified. Design proposals in [research.md](research.md) are the author's application of the evidence.

## S1. Current legal aid Act

- Title: আইনগত সহায়তা প্রদান আইন, ২০০০ (Legal Aid Services Act, 2000), consolidated text.
- Publisher: Bangladesh Laws; official copy distributed by the Directorate of Bangladesh Legal Aid.
- Date: original Act 26 January 2000; official consolidation bears 04/05/2026 and includes 2026 amendments.
- [Official consolidated PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/4/1c316c8c-ff1a-4cfa-863b-106072b00b0e.pdf).
- Pinpoints: section 2(b)(আ), PDF page 1; section 7(1)(গ), (ঝ), (ণ), PDF page 4; section 21A, PDF page 14.
- Use: statutory service scope, emergency support, referrals, digital access and database protection.
- Limit: it does not specify this proposed AI workflow, establish proxy authority merely through kinship, or settle every operative rule and territorial notification.

## S2. Current service context

- Title: National Legal Aid Day inaugurated to advance access to justice in Bangladesh.
- Publisher: UNDP Bangladesh, 28 April 2026.
- [Announcement](https://www.undp.org/bangladesh/press-releases/national-legal-aid-day-inaugurated-advance-access-justice-bangladesh).
- Pinpoint: opening paragraph.
- Use: identifies DBLA, ADLASB and 16699.
- Limit: project communication, not a service specification or evidence that an AI receptionist exists.

## S3. WHO first-line support

- Title: Responding to intimate partner violence and sexual violence against women: WHO clinical and policy guidelines.
- Publisher: World Health Organization, 2013.
- [Publication page](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548595); [guideline text](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/85240/9789241548595_eng.2013?sequence=1).
- Pinpoint: recommendation 1 and accompanying remarks, printed page 16.
- Use: privacy, confidentiality, supportive response and survivor decisions.
- Limit: healthcare guidance; does not prescribe Bangladesh legal-aid duties or authorize disclosure without local legal assessment.

## S4. UN essential services package

- Title: Essential Services Package for Women and Girls Subject to Violence: Core Elements and Quality Guidelines.
- Publishers: UN Women, UNFPA, WHO, UNDP and UNODC, 2015.
- [Publication page](https://www.unwomen.org/en/digital-library/publications/2015/12/essential-services-package-for-women-and-girls-subject-to-violence); [complete PDF](https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/pub-pdf/Essential_Services_Package_for_Women_and_Girls_Subject_to_Violence.pdf).
- Pinpoints: module 1, page 16 (informed consent and confidentiality); module 3, elements 2.1 to 2.3, pages 15 and 16; 3.3, page 18; 8.3 and 8.4, page 29; 10.4 and 10.5, page 32. In the combined PDF these are pages 17, 60, 61, 63, 74 and 77 respectively.
- Use: accessible intake, accurate recording, risk review and safe information sharing.
- Limit: international service guidance requiring local adaptation. It does not itself create domestic court powers.

## S5. NIST generative AI profile

- Title: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile, NIST AI 600-1.
- Publisher: US National Institute of Standards and Technology, July 2024.
- [Official PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf).
- Pinpoints: sections 2.2, 2.4, 2.6 and 2.7; suggested actions GV-5.1-002, MS-2.5-003 and MS-4.2-004.
- Use: confabulation, privacy, language disparities, disclosure of AI use, citation verification and monitoring overrides.
- Limit: voluntary risk guidance; it supplies neither a validated Bangla classifier nor Bangladesh legal rules.

## S6. WHO immediate-risk guidance

- Title: Health care for women subjected to intimate partner violence or sexual violence: a clinical handbook, WHO/RHR/14.26.
- Publisher: World Health Organization, 8 October 2014.
- [Publication page](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-14.26); [handbook PDF](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/136101/WHO_RHR_14.26_eng.pdf?sequence=1).
- Pinpoints: immediate-risk job aid, pages 25 and 26; pocket reminder, page 99.
- Use: worsening violence, weapon threats and strangulation as warning signs.
- Limit: the publication page and indexed official excerpts were available; direct PDF access was intermittent. This clinical tool has not been validated here for third-party Bangla telephone reports. Do not transplant its numerical threshold into AI triage.

## Retrieval cautions

The main Bangladesh Laws endpoint returned a gateway error during this review. The official consolidated PDF above was readable. Older judiciary copies still name the former Board in application-appeal provisions; they should not substitute for the current consolidation. The official law index also lists 2026 commencement notifications, which need separate examination for any claim about mandatory mediation. None of the sources establishes actual helpline staffing or a guaranteed response time.
