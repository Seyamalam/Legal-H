# Sources: fair case prioritization

All sources accessed on 5 September 2026. The three-factor mechanism in [research.md](research.md) is a proposal. No reviewed source establishes an existing Bangladesh legal-aid priority algorithm.

## S1. Current legal aid Act

- Title: আইনগত সহায়তা প্রদান আইন, ২০০০ (Legal Aid Services Act, 2000), consolidated text.
- Publisher: Bangladesh Laws; official copy distributed by DBLA.
- Date: original Act 26 January 2000; consolidation dated 04/05/2026, including 2026 amendments.
- [Official PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/4/1c316c8c-ff1a-4cfa-863b-106072b00b0e.pdf).
- Pinpoints: section 7(1)(গ) and (ণ), PDF page 4; section 16(1) and (2), PDF page 13.
- Use: emergency support and digital-access functions; direct and online application routes; rejection appeal to the Director General within 60 days.
- Limit: section 16 concerns applications and rejection, not an internal challenge to queue position. No three-factor weights, risk bands or response targets come from this provision.

## S2. UN essential services package

- Title: Essential Services Package for Women and Girls Subject to Violence: Core Elements and Quality Guidelines.
- Publishers: UN Women, UNFPA, WHO, UNDP and UNODC, 2015.
- [Official PDF](https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/pub-pdf/Essential_Services_Package_for_Women_and_Girls_Subject_to_Violence.pdf).
- Pinpoints: module 3, elements 2.1 to 2.3 (pages 15 and 16), 3.1 (page 17), 8.3 (page 29); combined PDF pages 60, 61, 62 and 74.
- Use: accessible contact, prompt response to violence and continuing risk assessment.
- Limit: international service-design guidance. It does not decide how every civil, family and criminal legal-aid application in Bangladesh must compare with every other application.

## S3. NIST AI Risk Management Framework

- Title: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), NIST AI 100-1.
- Publisher: US National Institute of Standards and Technology, 26 January 2023.
- [Official framework PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf); [core functions online](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/); [appendix C on human-AI interaction](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/appendices/app-c-ai-risk-management-and-human-ai-interaction/).
- Pinpoints: GOVERN 3.2; MEASURE 2.11 and 3.3; MANAGE 4.1; appendix C.
- Use: defined oversight roles, bias evaluation, feedback, appeal and override, monitoring, and examining human deference to AI.
- Limit: voluntary general guidance, not Bangladesh law. [NIST's framework page](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) reports that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised; this research cites the identified 2023 edition, not an unexamined future revision.

## S4. NIST bias guidance

- Title: AI Research: Identifying and Managing Harmful Bias in AI.
- Publisher: US National Institute of Standards and Technology.
- Date: created 6 April 2020; updated 7 February 2025. Links to NIST Special Publication 1270, released March 2022.
- [Official research page](https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence/ai-fundamental-research-managing-ai-bias).
- Use: explains why bias can arise beyond training-data balance and can scale through AI.
- Limit: does not test this proposed system or prove discrimination by any particular Bangladesh office. The recommendation to avoid historical approvals as an unexamined training target is a design inference.

## Sources not relied on for current legal propositions

Older [Sylhet judiciary Act text](https://sylhet.judiciary.gov.bd/bn/menu/page/the-legal-aid-act) and other district copies still reproduce section 16 with an appeal to the former Board. The current official consolidation identifies the Director General. An unchanged web-page title or a recent crawl date does not make an old legislative text current.

The [official law index](https://nlaso.portal.gov.bd/pages/laws) lists additional 2026 commencement notifications. The intake-priority proposal does not assume that all listed proceedings are subject to identical mediation obligations in every district.
