# Sources for challenge 1

Reviewed on 5 September 2026. English descriptions below are research translations or summaries of the Bangla text, except the older official English version. Page numbers refer to PDF pages unless a printed gazette page is specified.

## C1-S1: Consolidated Legal Aid Services Act, 2000

Publisher: BD Laws, Ministry of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs; copy hosted and linked by DBLA. The PDF bears a 4 May 2026 print date.

- [DBLA laws index](https://nlaso.gov.bd/pages/laws)
- [Official consolidated PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2026/4/1c316c8c-ff1a-4cfa-863b-106072b00b0e.pdf)
- [Canonical BD Laws page supplied in the question](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-details-834.html)

Verified: section 16(1), PDF p13, expressly permits direct and online applications to the Directorate's Supreme Court legal aid office or regional, district, and upazila legal aid offices. Section 16(2) provides an appeal to the Director General within 60 days of rejection. Footnote 59, PDF p17, attributes replacement of section 16 to section 24 of Act No. 45 of 2026.

Method: downloaded the DBLA-linked PDF, extracted text, and visually checked p13 because extracted Bangla loses characters. The canonical BD Laws endpoint returned connection/502 errors; it was not the text used for the finding. This May consolidation does not by itself establish the later territorial commencement of mediation provisions.

## C1-S2: Legal Aid Services (Amendment) Act, 2026

Publisher: Bangladesh Parliament, Bangladesh Gazette Extraordinary, 10 April 2026; Act No. 45 of 2026; printed pp15717-15739.

[Official gazette PDF](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/61406_96331.pdf)

Verified visually: section 24, PDF p18, printed p15734, substitutes section 16 with direct/online application language and the Director General appeal route. Section 1(2), PDF p1, reserves section 21B, section 21C, and the Schedule for commencement by date and area notification; section 1(3) brings the other provisions into force immediately. Section 35, PDF p23, repeals the 2025 amendment ordinance and 2026 second amendment ordinance, while saving notifications, actions, measures, and proceedings.

The gazette confirms the amending instrument and avoids confusing its section 24 with section 16 of the amended principal Act.

## C1-S3: Earlier official English Legal Aid Services Act

Publisher: Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division, Bangladesh Gazette, 16 October 2014; translation notification dated 13 October 2014. DBLA labels this the previous law.

[Official older English PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-nlaso/2024/12/e7930dc429ce4ea49673b68500c97872.pdf)

Section 16 directs applications to the Supreme Court, District, or Special Committee. It does not expressly specify online filing in that section. This supports a comparison of statutory wording, not a claim that online applications were formerly unlawful or technically unavailable. It is historical material, not the current law.

## C1-S4: DBLA online application service

Publisher: DBLA. Live page inspected on 5 September 2026.

- [DBLA homepage](https://nlaso.gov.bd/)
- [Online application](https://db.nlaso.gov.bd/Pages/OnlineApplications.aspx)

The homepage links to a structured application form. The form separates applicant and representative information. This establishes an existing digital entry point; it does not establish completed voice filing, autonomous eligibility decisions, or blanket proxy authority.
