# Sources

Access date for all entries: 5 September 2026. English descriptions of Bengali material are research paraphrases. Page numbers for the gazette distinguish PDF pages from printed pages. No district visit or live call is claimed.

## S1. DBLA service website

- Publisher: Bangladesh Legal Aid Department (DBLA), Government of Bangladesh.
- Title: হোম (Home).
- URL: [DBLA portal](https://nlaso.portal.gov.bd/).
- Date: dynamic page; search index showed September 2026 material. Earlier main-domain page fetches timed out.
- Location: internal e-services and national helpline call-centre sections.
- Supports: official online-application and legal-aid office-management links, and the 16699 number.
- Limit: public listings do not establish internal permissions, accessibility compliance or database integration. Discovery through indexed official content is weaker than an observed completed transaction.

## S3. Personal Data Protection Act, 2026

- Publisher: Bangladesh Parliament; official Bangladesh Gazette, hosted through the ICT Division.
- Title: ব্যক্তিগত উপাত্ত সুরক্ষা আইন, ২০২৬.
- URL: [ICT Division publication page](https://ictd.gov.bd/pages/laws/ব্যক্তিগত-উপাত্ত-সুরক্ষা-আইন-২০২৬-70is1t-69df5ba2210b1799cc640412), [official gazette PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-ictd/2026/3/1c355c6a-cc9c-42a0-b07f-e0876b89dc9d.pdf).
- Dates: gazette 10 April 2026; ICT upload 15 April 2026.
- Pinpoints: section 1(3), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 16436; section 5, PDF p. 7 / printed p. 16441; section 16, PDF p. 15 / printed p. 16449; section 17, PDF p. 16 / printed p. 16450; section 44, PDF p. 27 / printed p. 16461.
- Supports: current-law summary in the research notes, with commencement and consent qualifications.
- Verification: downloaded the official linked PDF and visually read rendered pages because the extracted Bengali text was corrupted.
- Limit: this is not a consolidated audit of later instruments or an official English translation. The proposed DLAS permissions are an application of the evidence, not statutory wording.

## S4. NNEDV texting and messaging guidance

- Publisher: National Network to End Domestic Violence, Safety Net Project.
- Title: Texting & Messaging with Survivors: Best Practices.
- URL: [Full guidance](https://www.techsafety.org/text-best-practices).
- Date: copyright 2019; no separate revision date stated.
- Pinpoints: "Minimize Interception," "Prevent Impersonation," "Ensure Data Privacy" and "Data Security for Hotlines or Messaging-Based Advocacy."
- Supports: the privacy and impersonation risks summarized in the research notes.
- Limit: United States survivor-service guidance. Its references to federal/state disclosure obligations and evidence handling cannot simply become DBLA rules. Avoid mechanically deleting records that a lawful case-retention policy requires.

## S5. Wesnet phone communication guidance

- Publisher: Wesnet, Safety Net Australia Service; adapted under licence from NNEDV.
- Title: Communicating with Survivors via Phone.
- URL: [Phone communication](https://techsafety.org.au/resources/technology-safety-agencies/phone-communication/).
- Date: displayed page timestamp 11 February 2025.
- Pinpoints: sections 1 "Calling survivors," 2 "Leaving voicemail messages," 3 "Dropped calls," and 5 "Collecting caller's information."
- Supports: survivor-agreed callback arrangements and awareness of records outside the case database.
- Limit: Australian practice guidance, not a legal standard for Bangladesh. A blocked caller ID is not a guarantee that a call leaves no trace.

## S6. NIST access-control guidance

- Publisher: United States National Institute of Standards and Technology.
- Title: Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems and Organizations, SP 800-171 revision 3.
- URL: [Full publication](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/800-171r3/NIST.SP.800-171r3.html).
- Date: May 2024.
- Pinpoints: 03.01.01 account management; 03.01.02 access enforcement; 03.01.03 information-flow enforcement; 03.01.04 separation of duties; 03.01.05 least privilege.
- Supports: authorize only task-necessary access, enforce that policy in systems, review privileges and remove obsolete permissions.
- Limit: its stated regulatory context is United States controlled unclassified information. Here it supplies a technical design reference, not a claim that DBLA must comply with this US framework.

## S7. WCAG 2.2

- Publisher: World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).
- Title: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2.
- URL: [Dated recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/).
- Date: 12 December 2024.
- Pinpoints: 2.1.1 keyboard; 3.1.1 language of page; 3.3.8 accessible authentication (minimum); 4.1.2 name, role, value.
- Supports: recognized web accessibility requirements relevant to the online alternative.
- Limit: WCAG applies to web content. It neither certifies a telephone flow nor proves a legal mandate to adopt WCAG in this deployment. Use testing to check the complete user journey.

## S8. Accessible authentication explanation

- Publisher: W3C Web Accessibility Initiative.
- Title: Understanding Success Criterion 3.3.8: Accessible Authentication (Minimum).
- URL: [Understanding 3.3.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html).
- Date: maintained explanatory page; use the access date rather than infer a publication date from a crawler.
- Pinpoint: "Two-factor authentication systems (verification codes)."
- Supports: a flow requiring manual transcription of a verification code needs an alternative or assistance such as paste/autofill to satisfy this criterion.
- Limit: not a blanket prohibition on OTPs and not evidence that voice biometrics or caller ID provides adequate identity assurance.

## S9. Programmatic names and controls

- Publisher: W3C Web Accessibility Initiative.
- Title: Understanding Success Criterion 4.1.2: Name, Role, Value.
- URL: [Understanding 4.1.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/name-role-value.html).
- Date: maintained explanatory page; publication date not established.
- Pinpoint: intent and benefits sections.
- Supports: controls must expose their function and state to assistive technology.
- Limit: visual Bangla text alone is not proof that an accessible name is available, and accessible names alone do not prove usable speech output.

## S10. Page-language support

- Publisher: W3C Web Accessibility Initiative.
- Title: Understanding Success Criterion 3.1.1: Language of Page.
- URL: [Understanding 3.1.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/language-of-page.html).
- Date: maintained explanatory page; publication date not established.
- Pinpoint: intent and benefits sections.
- Supports: a programmatically declared page language helps speech software apply appropriate pronunciation rules.
- Limit: declaring Bangla cannot install a missing voice engine or fix every device limitation described in the case.
