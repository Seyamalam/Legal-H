# Research notes

Research date: 5 September 2026. The findings below distinguish the supplied scenario, published rules and proposed DLAS behavior. Source details are in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Recommended focus

Choose one item: **the safe-contact timing note in Moyuri's communication history**, specifically the time when she can speak without being overheard. A phone number or address would also be defensible, but the timing note answers the scenario more precisely. Someone who learns it could interrupt the only private conversation she has, monitor her movements or infer that she is seeking help. These are risks inferred from the [case study](../case-study.md), not allegations about an actual DLAS breach.

Keep the answer about this one item. The surrounding phone ownership, postal address and notification rules explain why protecting that item matters; they need not become additional selected categories.

## Official service baseline

DBLA's website lists online applications and the 16699 national legal-aid helpline. Its November 2025 notice identifies 16699 as the new toll-free number. These are suitable official references for a proposal to let Ripon use a telephone route. Neither the public listings nor the notice proves that the helpline currently offers voice authentication, a protected contact-preference editor or integration with a shared case record. Present those functions as improvements. [S1, S2](sources.md#s1-dbla-service-website)

## Bangladesh legal position

The Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 is Act No. 63, gazetted on 10 April 2026. Section 44 repeals the 2025 Ordinance and its 2026 amendment. Section 1(3) deems most provisions effective from 6 November 2025; section 23 and sections 31-35 have deferred commencement. Section 5(2) requires voluntary, specific, clear and withdrawable consent, with information about purpose and retention; section 5(3) permits specified alternative processing grounds. Section 16 restricts disclosure for other purposes, subject to the Act's other provisions. Section 17 requires proportionate technical and organizational safeguards against unauthorized access or disclosure and includes encryption. The statute supports the proposal, but does not prescribe this role matrix. These are reading notes from the Bengali gazette, not an official English translation. [S3](sources.md#s3-personal-data-protection-act-2026)

Do not describe consent as the only possible legal basis for every case-management operation. Do not promise absolute confidentiality against every lawful demand. Do not mistake Ripon's blindness, Moyuri's lack of internet experience or dependence on relatives for an inability to make decisions. A family connection alone does not establish the scope of authorized disclosure. The proposed service should ask Moyuri directly when it is safe to do so and record which actions Ripon may take.

## Evidence about survivor communications

NNEDV's Safety Net guidance warns that messages and their dates can remain on devices or backups and that someone else may impersonate the survivor. It recommends agreeing an identity check, reviewing communication safety and keeping little information on staff devices. This is specialist practice guidance from the United States, not Bangladeshi law. [S4](sources.md#s4-nnedv-texting-and-messaging-guidance)

Wesnet advises workers to agree safe calling times, voicemail content and what to do after a dropped call. It also points to call logs and billing accounts as disclosure routes. These practical risks apply to the scenario even if the database itself uses encryption. [S5](sources.md#s5-wesnet-phone-communication-guidance)

## Proposed access policy

The following is a design proposal. DLAS's actual internal permissions were not inspected.

| Person or role | Access to the timing note | Reason and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moyuri | Review and request changes through a safe, verified route | The note concerns her safety and should reflect her current choice. |
| Assigned legal-aid officer and designated caseworker | Read and update for this case | They need it to arrange safe contact. Record who confirmed each change. |
| Assigned lawyer | Read only when responsible for contacting her | Assignment alone should not open unrelated private communications. |
| Ripon | Only the particular view or update permission Moyuri authorizes | His phone number and his help with an application do not create unrestricted access. |
| Mediator | Use staff-arranged contact by default | Do not copy the private timing note into a shared mediation record or invite. |
| Other lawyers, applicants, opposing parties and unrelated staff | No access | They have no operational reason to see this note. |
| Technical administrators | No routine readable access | Any exceptional support access should need approval, a recorded reason and an expiry time. |

A supervisor may grant a narrowly defined exception when a documented legal or urgent operational basis warrants it. The system should log that decision for review. A generic administrator account must not become a permanent exception.

## How technology would enforce it

Use a separate protected field for the timing note. At each read or write, the server should check the user's identity, permitted role, active case assignment and, for Ripon, the scope and expiry of his authorization. Deny requests that fail any check. Hiding a field in the webpage is insufficient if an API, downloaded document, search result or export still reveals it.

Encrypt stored data and network traffic. Keep decryption permissions separate from routine technical administration. Record successful and refused access, edits and exceptional approvals in protected audit logs, but do not duplicate the timing note into those logs. Review suspicious access and remove permissions when a case assignment ends or Moyuri withdraws the relevant proxy authorization. These choices apply NIST's least-privilege and access-enforcement principles; NIST does not impose Bangladeshi legal duties. [S6](sources.md#s6-nist-access-control-guidance)

The notification service should use the verified contact plan. It should not infer permission for texts from a telephone-number field, infer a mailing address from a household address, or treat last Friday's private interval as permanent permission to call every Friday. A missed call should follow the agreed plan rather than trigger an automatic callback, voicemail or SMS.

## One change Ripon can use independently

Propose a **Bangla telephone flow for reviewing or requesting changes to the protected contact preference** through 16699. Ripon can already dial and answer a call. The flow should read the relevant authorized information aloud, offer repeat and correction, accept spoken choices or keypad input, and confirm what it recorded. He should not have to open a separate app, read a PDF, solve a visual CAPTCHA or transcribe an SMS code. A staff member can verify authorization and approve a change within that telephone interaction. Independence here means that he can complete his part without a sighted helper beside him; it does not mean eliminating the human caseworker.

Do not present a recorded voice or caller ID as conclusive identity evidence. A prearranged check may support a worker's verification, but the service must allow another route if the check is forgotten, compromised or unsafe. Ripon cannot authenticate as Moyuri merely because he calls from the number in her file. A proposed update can remain pending until a worker confirms it with Moyuri through the existing safe route. The service should say that the request is pending rather than imply the new contact time is approved.

An accessible web form would also help other users. WCAG 2.2 provides criteria for labels exposed to assistive technology, page language, keyboard use and accessible authentication. W3C's authentication guidance addresses forced code transcription; it does not require a voice call or establish that a particular telephone process is secure. [S7-S10](sources.md#s7-wcag-22)

For Ripon specifically, changing Bangla labels alone cannot establish independence: the scenario says his device often stays silent on Bangla controls, and he needs help opening a recording app. Test the proposed telephone step with blind Bangla speakers and with the actual device capabilities described in the case.

## What remains unverified

- DBLA's internal role definitions, data-retention schedule, export restrictions and call-recording practice.
- A live helpline workflow for proxy consent and safe-contact changes. No call was placed and no district office was visited.
- Rules, regulations, exemptions or operational directions that would alter the application of the 2026 Act to a particular DLAS deployment.
- Whether a particular Bangla text-to-speech engine, device or telephone menu is usable by Ripon. Standards compliance and user testing answer different questions.
