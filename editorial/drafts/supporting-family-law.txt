# Supporting family and dowry law

Primary-source checks for Challenge 4, completed on 5 September 2026. These notes distinguish a maintenance claim, a dowry-demand offence and injury inflicted for dowry. English explanations of Bangla legislation are working summaries, unless the source is identified as an authentic English text.

## Maintenance is a separate claim

Section 5 of the Family Courts Act, 2023 gives the Family Court jurisdiction over dissolution of marriage, restitution of conjugal rights, dower, maintenance, and guardianship and custody of children, subject to the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961. Maintenance is section 5(ঘ), conventionally cited in English as section 5(d). Section 6(1) also permits dissolution, dower and maintenance proceedings where the wife ordinarily resides. [Official Gazette, PDF page 3, printed page 13375](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/50340_46458.pdf).

For the scenario, Moyuri's and her daughter's support needs should be assessed separately from the husband's demand for a motorcycle. A maintenance agreement should state the amount, payment dates and how compliance will be checked, if a lawyer concludes that negotiation is safe and appropriate. It must not make food or child support conditional on paying the demanded dowry or withdrawing unrelated allegations. This is a design and legal-triage inference; section 5 itself establishes jurisdiction, not a particular monthly award.

## A dowry demand is not an enforceable debt

The Dowry Prohibition Act, 2018 defines dowry to include property demanded as a condition of continuing a marriage. Its definition excludes Muslim dower or mehr. Section 3 criminalises a demand; section 5 makes a dowry agreement void. Sections 7 and 8 make offences cognizable, non-bailable and compoundable, with criminal procedure applying. [Authentic English text, sections 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8, PDF pages 2 and 3](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-legislativediv/2024/12/049530ae1cf74e4a91122d91eca802a0.pdf).

The proposed motorcycle condition may fit that definition, depending on the evidence. The system should record the words used and refer classification to a lawyer. It must not calculate a "fair" motorcycle payment or draft a settlement that makes the demand lawful. Compoundability concerns the handling of an offence through the proper process. It does not validate the prohibited bargain or make pressured consent reliable.

## Hurt inflicted for dowry has its own classification

Section 11 of the Women and Children Repression Prevention Act, 2000 addresses death, attempted death, grievous hurt and simple hurt caused for dowry by a husband or the specified people acting on his side. Section 11(c) concerns simple hurt. The dowry connection and injury classification matter; a report of beatings and a separate demand do not establish every element without further inquiry. [BD Laws, section 11](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-835.html).

The 2026 amendment, Act No. 85, replaces the 2025 amendment ordinance. Section 1(2) deems it effective from 25 March 2025. Section 6(c) sets section 11(c)'s imprisonment range at two to five years. Section 26 inserts section 35: that offence is cognizable, non-bailable and compoundable, ordinarily before a First Class Magistrate under criminal procedure and evidence law. There are exceptions for pending cases and offences joined with matters tried by a tribunal. Section 27 preserves actions under the repealed ordinance. [2026 Gazette, sections 1, 6, 26 and 27, PDF pages 1, 4, 10 and 11](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/61238_32270.pdf).

This is not a rule that all domestic abuse can be compromised. A legal-aid officer must identify the alleged offence and the correct forum before deciding whether a lawful settlement route exists. Nor does legal compoundability establish that ODR is safe for this woman. The interface should support a private assessment of coercion, injury and retaliation risk, rather than automatically presenting a settlement button whenever it detects section 11(c).

The published amendment date is 10 April 2026. Its deemed commencement is earlier. Any account of transition or pending cases must use the commencement provision and relevant saving clauses; it should not assume that every case filed before April 2026 remains with a tribunal.
