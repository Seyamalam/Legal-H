## Matters still requiring verification

The domestic-violence materials need particular care. A February government announcement refers to a 2026 ordinance, while an official September notice calls a meeting to review a draft 2026 Act. This research did not locate a promulgated replacement instrument or the outcome of that meeting. The [status note](domestic-violence-law-status.md) records the conflicting evidence and the verified 2010 provisions without claiming to have resolved the current consolidation.

Before implementing the proposal, DBLA would need to confirm Friday or out-of-hours coverage, accepted missing-document procedures, proxy authorization, a safe authentication method for shared phones, and any approved domestic digital signing procedure. The question-specific research files identify further operational checks. These gaps do not justify presenting an unbuilt feature as an existing service.

The research involved public documents and read-only inspection of the application form. No office visit, helpline call, submitted application, screen-reader usability test or participant interview took place. The event dates in the supplied questions remain attributed to that text.

## How to use the source files

Read each `sources.md` alongside its `research.md`. Source entries distinguish verified legal text, service information, historical comparisons and unresolved leads. Bangla legal provisions are summarized in English; those summaries are not official translations. Relevant PDF pages were visually checked where text extraction was unreliable.

The source registers link to official documents instead of reproducing complete publications. External links can change, and a successful page response alone does not establish that its law or service information remains current.
