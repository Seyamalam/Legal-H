# Challenge 3: justice Operations

Word limit: 300 words.

Read the [shared guidelines](../guidelines.md) and [case study](../case-study.md). The [original supplied text](../original-screening-text.txt) is the reference for exact wording. This copy removes form controls and normalizes punctuation.

Scenario: Ripon's application reaches the Joypurhat District Legal Aid Office on a working day, along with a large number of others. Some of those files need immediate attention based on urgency, legal merit, vulnerability, or likelihood of requiring prompt intervention compared to other filed cases without harming anyone. The office wants to introduce a digital system that helps officers see which cases may require immediate action.

If you were designing the system to support the District Legal Aid Officer (DLAO), how would you design a fair and transparent mechanism for assessing the relative priority/merit of incoming cases? Choose THREE factors and explain: 1. What factors should the DLAO consider when assessing the priority of a case? 2. How could those factors be captured and represented digitally? 3. How could the system help the DLAO compare or prioritize cases without automatically replacing the DLAO's legal judgment? 4. What safeguard would you introduce to prevent an applicant from being unfairly deprioritized because of incomplete information, vulnerability, or limited digital access?
