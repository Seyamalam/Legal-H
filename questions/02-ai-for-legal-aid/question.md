# Challenge 2: aI for Legal Aid

Word limit: 300 words.

Read the [shared guidelines](../guidelines.md) and [case study](../case-study.md). The [original supplied text](../original-screening-text.txt) is the reference for exact wording. This copy removes form controls and normalizes punctuation.

Scenario: Ripon's call is answered by a future Digital Legal Aid System (DLAS) AI assistant that works in Bangla, by voice. He says:

"আমার বোনের স্বামী তাকে মারধর করে, পাঁচ মাস ধরে কোনো খরচও দেয় না। বোনের কাছে ফোন নাই, আমি চোখে দেখি না। আমরা এখন কী করতে পারি?"

The AI assistant has general legal information and whatever intake information has been captured so far. It has nothing else, and the applicant herself is not on the line.

Your team must explain which part of this interaction could reasonably be handled by AI, and at what point the matter must be transferred to a human legal aid officer or panel lawyer.

Give one proposed AI-assisted workflow, from Ripon's first interaction to human intervention. Your workflow should also identify any situation in which the AI should trigger an urgent human review. Also, your proposed workflow must treat all information provided by Ripon as third-party information unless and until it can be safely verified with Moyuri. Your answer must include: ● What AI can do ● What AI should not do ● When escalation occurs ● What information should be captured for the human officer, given that the person the case is about never speaks to the system directly
