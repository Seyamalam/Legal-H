# Research and reference guidelines

Round-1 Screening Questions
Guidelines for Research & Reference
Participants are encouraged to conduct their own research before responding to the round-1 questions. As the Innovation Challenge focuses on the intersection of national laws, government legal aid, technology and digital service delivery, teams should, while solving the challenges, provide references of authentic sources.

Participants are particularly encouraged to review:

DBLA Official Website : for online application, National Legal Aid Helpline : 16699, complaint / legal advice, Citizen Charter / Service Commitment, contact information, legal-aid-related information and publication, links to relevant government/legal institutions.
Laws of Bangladesh / BD Laws : for the Legal Aid Services Act, 2000 and its amendments, as well as other relevant laws and legal provisions.
Note: Please solve the issues in a way where AI may only provide relevant primary support, but human-centric intervention is required for final legal assistance and case-management decisions.

Applies to all six questions: Before answering, please review the relevant legal aid services, forms and information available on the official DBLA website and mention the source that informed your answer. You are also encouraged to visit your district's Legal Aid Office and observe its activities.
