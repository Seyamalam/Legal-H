# Challenge 6: aI-Assisted Call Centre and Omnichannel Support

Word limit: 250 words.

Read the [shared guidelines](../guidelines.md) and [case study](../case-study.md). The [original supplied text](../original-screening-text.txt) is the reference for exact wording. This copy removes form controls and normalizes punctuation.

Scenario: Ripon calls 16699 from the button phone at the tea stall and is told that his sister may qualify for legal aid and that an application can also be made digitally. Later he goes the nearby Union Digital Center where the entrepreneur guides him to try the online route on the handed down smartphone.


Based on your review of the DBLA website and the current legal-aid application process, identify ONE specific element that may create difficulty for a citizen. This could be: •a step in the application process; •an information requirement; •a document requirement; •a form filled; or •another specific feature of the existing process. Explain why this particular element may create difficulty for a citizen and propose ONE technology-enabled improvement that would make the process easier without removing or changing any legally or administratively necessary requirement. Your solution has to hold up under three conditions: the citizen has no working internet, the person filing cannot read a screen, and nothing may be pushed to Moyuri's own handset that could put her at risk at home.
